v1.3.2 — security update
• Protection no longer waits for the first unlock after a restart: wrong PINs (B1, A11), B4, the power-button gesture (C3), USB while locked (A9), C4, C6 and a queued wipe work from boot (Device Owner).
• Their settings move to device-protected storage; the App PIN and secrets stay encrypted.
• No countdown in the first 5 minutes after a restart.
• Before the first unlock, Cancel opens the unlock screen.
